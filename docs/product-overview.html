PickMyPlate
Product overview

Every child can choose their own lunch.

A picture-based, read-aloud lunch menu for children in special educational needs (SEND) schools, with allergen-aware diet cards and portion counts for the kitchen.

VersionPrototype 0.1
DateSeptember 2026
TypeWeb app (works in any browser)
PriceFree and open source
Why it exists

The problem

In SEND schools, many children are autistic, non-verbal, or can't yet read. A written lunch menu doesn't work for them, so an adult often chooses for them. That takes away independence and can cause anxiety at mealtimes.

At the same time, school kitchens must manage the UK's 14 allergens and many individual diets safely, often with paper lists.

For children

Written menus are hard to read and understand. Unfamiliar food causes anxiety. Children have little say in what they eat.

For kitchens

Allergen checks rely on memory and paper. It's hard to know how many portions to cook, which leads to food waste.

The solution

PickMyPlate turns choosing lunch into something every child can do on their own:

FeatureWhat it does
Big picture cardsEach dish is shown as a large picture (emoji or a real photo of the school's food).
Read aloudTapping a dish says its name out loud, so children who can't read can still choose.
"Now and Next" stepsOne course at a time (Main → Pudding → All done), using a routine SEND schools already know.
Confirm stepA clear "I choose…" button, so one accidental tap doesn't count.
Diet cardsColour-coded cards (e.g. No milk No eggs) hide unsafe dishes. Cards use colours, never children's names.
14 UK allergensKitchen staff tag each dish with the allergens it contains.
Portion countsAnonymous counts show how many of each dish to prepare, which reduces waste.

Choose lunch

The screen children use, usually on a tablet with an adult beside them.

The child sees large pictures of today's mains. The yellow "Now: Main" badge shows where they are.

How to use it

  1. Pick a diet card (adult). Tap the child's colour card at the top, or leave "No card". Unsafe dishes disappear.
  2. Choose a main (child). The child taps a picture. It gets a yellow border and tick, and the app says its name aloud.
  3. Confirm. The child taps the green "I choose…" button. "Say it" repeats the name.
  4. Choose a pudding. Same as before. "Back" returns to the main.
  5. All done. Both choices are shown and read aloud. The adult taps "Next child" to reset.
Screen 2 of 3 · For kitchen staff

Kitchen menu

Where staff set up each day's menu and the diet cards.

Red labels show allergens. Green labels show Vegetarian, Vegan or Halal.

Today's menu

  • Add a dish: name, course, picture or photo, allergens and dietary tags.
  • Edit any dish, or Remove it (tap twice to confirm).

Diet cards and settings

  • Add card: choose a colour and the allergens to avoid, with an optional "Vegetarian only".
  • Each card shows how many dishes it allows.
  • Turn reading aloud on or off.

Behind the scenes

Privacy and safety

  • No personal data. No children's names or medical records are stored. Diet cards use colours and codes only.
  • Stays on the device. Data is saved only in the browser being used. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • People stay in charge. Allergen information must always be checked against the kitchen's official records.
  • Prototype status. Currently tested with sample data only.
